Privacy Policy TubeAIO-NextGen

📅 Effective Date: June 16, 2026

Welcome to TubeAIO-NextGen (“we,” “our,” or “us”). Your privacy is fundamental to our mission. This Privacy Policy explains in detail how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application (the “App”). By installing or using TubeAIO-NextGen, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

🔐 At a glance: We minimize data collection. We do not sell your personal data. Your content remains under your control, and we employ strong safeguards to protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in several ways to provide, improve, and secure TubeAIO-NextGen. The categories include:

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account & Profile Data: If you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, username, and profile preferences. This is optional for certain features.
  • User Content: When you use AI-powered tools (e.g., video analysis, content generation, summaries), we process the text, prompts, uploaded files, and metadata you submit. This content is used solely to deliver the requested functionality.
  • Communications: If you contact support at contact@tubeaio.com, we collect your email address, message content, and any attachments to resolve your inquiry.
  • Feedback & Surveys: Voluntary feedback, ratings, or survey responses you submit.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, mobile network information, browser type (if applicable), and time zone settings.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Features accessed, taps, clicks, session duration, feature usage frequency, crash logs, and performance diagnostics.
  • AI Processing Telemetry (Anonymized): To improve our AI models, we may collect anonymized usage patterns (e.g., latency, error rates). No raw user content is tied to identifiable telemetry without explicit consent.
  • IP Address & Approximate Location: Derived from your connection for security, fraud prevention, and regional compliance. We do not collect precise GPS location unless explicitly granted for a specific feature (e.g., localized recommendations).

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Authentication Partners: If you sign in via Google, Apple, or other SSO providers, we receive a token and basic profile information (name, email) as permitted by your privacy settings with that provider.
  • Analytics & Crash Reporting: Tools like Firebase, Sentry, or similar provide aggregated insights and crash data, strictly under data processing agreements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To operate, maintain, and provide TubeAIO-NextGen’s AI-driven features, including personalized content generation and analysis.
  • Personalization: To tailor your experience, remember preferences, and offer relevant suggestions.
  • Improvement & Research: Analyze aggregated, de-identified data to enhance AI models, fix bugs, and develop new functionalities.
  • Customer Support: Respond to your messages, troubleshoot issues, and send service-related announcements.
  • Safety & Security: Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security incidents, and harmful activities. Verify accounts and enforce our terms.
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or enforceable governmental requests.
  • Marketing (with consent): If you opt in, we may send newsletters or promotional content about TubeAIO-NextGen. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers & Sub-processors: Trusted third parties that assist with cloud hosting, AI model inference, analytics, customer support, and email delivery. They are bound by strict data protection agreements.
  • Business Transfers: If TubeAIO-NextGen is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred with prior notice and continued protection.
  • Legal Obligations: When required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or our company.
  • With Your Consent: We may share data for additional purposes when you explicitly agree.
  • Aggregated/De-identified Data: We may share non-personal, statistical data for research or marketing purposes.

4. AI Data Processing & User Content

TubeAIO-NextGen leverages advanced artificial intelligence models. Please understand:

  • Your Content is Yours: You retain all ownership of the prompts, videos, text, and files you input. We do not claim ownership over your generated outputs.
  • Processing Purity: We process your content transiently to generate results. We do not use your private content to train our base AI models unless you explicitly join a model improvement program (opt-in).
  • Human Review: In rare cases (debugging, safety enforcement), anonymized snippets may be reviewed by authorized personnel bound by confidentiality. Automated systems are the default.
  • Third-Party AI Providers: Some features may rely on trusted external AI APIs (e.g., OpenAI, cloud AI services). Data sent to these providers is subject to their stringent privacy and data usage policies, and we ensure zero-retention agreements where possible.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined:

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active. Upon deletion request, we remove or anonymize your data within 30 days, unless legal retention applies.
  • User Content & AI Interactions: Stored for the duration needed to provide the service (e.g., history) and automatically purged after 90 days of inactivity, unless you save items explicitly.
  • Analytics & Logs: Aggregated logs kept up to 24 months; crash logs and security logs may be kept longer for safety purposes.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you may have these rights:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of your personal data in a structured format.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): Request deletion of your data.
  • Restrict or Object: Limit processing or object to certain data uses, including direct marketing.
  • Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it anytime.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

To exercise any right, contact us at contact@tubeaio.com with subject “Privacy Rights Request”. We verify identity before processing requests.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Regular security audits, penetration testing, and vulnerability scanning.
  • Strict access controls and multi-factor authentication for internal systems.
  • Data minimization and pseudonymization where feasible.

However, no method is 100% secure. We encourage you to protect your account credentials.

8. Children’s Privacy

TubeAIO-NextGen is not directed to individuals under 16 years of age (or relevant age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will promptly delete it. Parents/guardians can contact us at contact@tubeaio.com.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside your residence. We ensure adequate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions) to protect data during such transfers, in compliance with applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you via in-app notice or email at least 14 days before material changes take effect. The “Effective Date” above indicates the latest revision. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
